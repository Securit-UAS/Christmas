HOW TO PLAY

Minimal administration. Maximum nonsense.

  1. Pick a challenge and tap Claim on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the Joyeux Noel group, send the prepared message and add your photograph or video separately.
  3. Videos submitted as evidence must be under 60 seconds. The AI referee will not judge audio or sound; anything dependent on speech, music or pronunciation needs human confirmation.
  4. Help other people score: react to their WhatsApp claims, or (preferably) reply CONFIRMED if you can genuinely verify them. Two confirmations are preferred, or the organiser can explicitly approve uncontested claims.
  5. Participation matters: if someone repeatedly ignores other people’s claims, the referee may deduct 5 points for that day, after reviewing the available evidence. No automatic penalties for missing reactions, being busy or not witnessing a claim. Myles is completely exempt.
  6. Family missions score towards households; individual challenges score for participants. Shared evidence can earn both participant points and approved equal household bonuses. Group-voted competitions are decided by a recorded human vote, not posting order.
  7. The daily chat export is adjudicated and the approved results published to the site.

WhatsApp sharing cannot automatically select your group or attach a photo. A claim remains provisional until the daily review.
